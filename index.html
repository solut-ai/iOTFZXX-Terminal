Two steps, in order. The dry run validates the full settlement plan on screen and signs nothing. The Connect & Sign step below it is the live, terminal-directed transaction: once a dry run passes, you connect the wallet that controls SettleMint Trust A, the terminal verifies that wallet and the BSC network, then directs an MUSDV → BNB swap backed by the Allowance Holder balance and the 721 NFT for you to sign in your wallet — and, once the swap confirms, directs the delivery to Found. No secret key or key material is ever held in this page; signing happens only in your wallet, and your wallet's own confirmation is the final check before anything is sent.

Connect searches this page for your browser wallet provider (the same wallet you use with ThirdWeb / MAP Secure Connect, client dbc43f4d2bbd9ede24fe4b4a0cd6ea99) and opens the connection request in it. If no wallet answers — for example, when this terminal is opened inside another app's embedded view, which a wallet cannot reach — the panel under the Connect button explains how to open the terminal in your wallet's browser instead. Signing runs a real swap from the connected wallet: if the swap router does not yet hold a spending approval for your MUSDV, your wallet will first ask you to approve it, then ask you to sign the swap itself. Check the amounts and network in each wallet confirmation before you approve. The BNB → USD off-ramp and the USD push to the Found bank account are directed by the terminal once the swap confirms on-chain; the bank leg settles through the Found receiver's own banking, not on BSC.
